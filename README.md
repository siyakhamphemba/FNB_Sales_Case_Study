Project Overview

Analysis of daily sales data for a retail product over 3+ years to derive key business insights, calculate metrics, identify promotional periods, and measure price elasticity.

The project was completed using multiple tools including Databricks, SQL, Power BI, Excel, Looker Studio, Lovable, and Draw.io.

Columns Date, Sales, Cost of Sales, Quantity Sold

# Metric Result
1 Daily Unit Price Sales ÷ Quantity Sold

5a 3 Promotional Periods Dec 2014, Jul 2015, Jan 2016
5b Price Elasticity of Demand -1.80 (ELASTIC)

Key Findings

# Finding
1 Total Revenue: R186.91M
2 Total Units Sold: 5.28M
3 Average Unit Price: R37.25
4 Gross Profit %: -3.81% (Negative)
5 Best Day: Saturday (R245K avg sales)
6 Best Month: December
7 Product is ELASTIC (promotions drive sales growth)
8 Budget category has best margins (-3.2%)

Key Insights

1. Product is ELASTIC - Promotions drive sales growth (Avg Elasticity: -1.80)
2. Negative margins (-3.81%) - Pricing review needed
3. Saturday peak - Focus marketing efforts on Saturdays
4. December peak - Seasonal opportunity
5. Budget category - Better margins (-3.2% vs premium -6.8%)

---

Recommendations

# Recommendation Expected Impact
1 Increase base price by 10-15% Improve margins
2 Run targeted promotions during peak seasons Increase revenue
3 Focus marketing on Saturdays Maximize sales
4 Review cost structure Improve profitability
5 Focus on Budget category Increase profit margin
6 Consider product bundling strategies Boost revenue

Conclusion

The FNB Sales Case Study analysis successfully achieved all project objectives. The analysis revealed that the product generates significant revenue (R186.91M) but operates at negative margins (-3.81%). The product is price elastic, meaning that promotional pricing effectively drives sales volume growth.
